# EProject Manifest

## Mission

The mission of this project is to explore startup opportunities in the field of european tech sovereignty.

## Requirements

1. EProject is an european startup AI project, based in Europe and designed for Europe.
2. EProject aims to help Europe to stand on its own feet, to strenghten the sub-developed tech sectors and become independent from other markets.
3. EProject will seek financing through the EU funding programs and venture capital investments.
4. Any AI project that aligns with EProject's mission and can kickoff with a low capital, strong moat, and sustainable business model is a valid EProject.
