## Bottom line

The move away from Microsoft 365 is real and accelerating. About 100,000 public-sector workplaces use openDesk. France has made its own Visio tool mandatory across state services by 2027. France, Germany and the Netherlands are co-developing their suites. The manifest needs an AI project, so the best entry is an **AI assistant layer that runs inside the sovereign suites on EU models**. **Migration tooling** and **enterprise admin and compliance add-ons** complete the shortlist. Hosting openDesk as a certified operator is out. It needs BSI C5 and SEAL level 3 attestations, which fails the capital test.

## 2. The shortlisted problems in detail

### G2 — AI assistant layer for sovereign suites

- **Who pays today.** Administrations that leave Microsoft 365 also leave Copilot. Their staff still expect AI drafting and search **[assumed]**.
- **Why existing solutions fail.** The suites are assemblies of separate open-source products. openDesk bundles Nextcloud, Collabora, OpenProject, XWiki, Element, OX and Jitsi **[web]**. An assistant that works across all of them does not exist yet **[assumed]**.
- **What AI changed.** European open-weight models can now run on EU compute at acceptable quality. The Mistral funding round in Sep 2026 confirms the model layer is mature: €3B at €21B valuation, co-led by the Scaleup Europe Fund **[web]**.
- **The asset after five years.** Connectors across every suite component, plus evaluation sets for public-sector tasks in each language.
- **Who already competes.** Nextcloud ships its own assistant **[reviewer]**. DINUM's Albert programme supplies sovereign AI to the French state **[reviewer]**. The gap is in cross-suite integration and in German and Dutch deployments **[assumed]**.

### G1 — Migration tooling

- **Who pays today.** Every administration that switches pays for the migration. Schleswig-Holstein moved about 80% of its workstations **[sourced, not rechecked]**. France expects to move hundreds of thousands of agents by 2027, though reported figures conflict **[web]**.
- **What AI changed.** Models can map permissions, convert documents and archive chat history at scale, with less manual cleanup **[assumed]**.
- **The weakness.** The moat is thin, because migrations are one-off projects. Use it as the entry that brings in G2 and G5 customers.

### G5 — Enterprise admin and compliance add-ons

- **Who pays today.** Regulated enterprises cannot leave Microsoft 365 without retention, eDiscovery and data-loss prevention **[reviewer]**. Since 23 Sep 2026, private providers can build offerings on openDesk Enterprise **[web]**, which opens private-sector demand.

## 3. Entry decisions

- **First buyer.** German state and municipal administrations on openDesk, then private-sector openDesk customers through the ZenDiS partner programme. The programme has distributor and system-integrator roles **[web]**.
- **First countries.** Germany through openDesk. France through La Suite, where the state builds in-house and buys less **[assumed]**. The Netherlands through Mijn Bureau **[web]**.
- **Regulatory clock.** The EU Open Source Strategy is policy, not law **[sourced]**. France's Visio mandate has a 2027 deadline and blocks Teams, Zoom and Google Meet on the state network **[web]**. A planned European Digital Infrastructure Consortium (EDIC) called Digital Commons would formalise co-development **[web]**.
- **Competitive response.** The state bodies own the core products, ZenDiS for openDesk and DINUM for La Suite. They may build AI features themselves. G2 must therefore be built as an open-source add-on that they can adopt, not as a closed fork **[assumed]**.
- **Funding.** DIGITAL, national sovereignty programmes and the EIC. French ministries were asked for about €4M toward La Suite's €12M 2026 budget **[web]**, which shows the public budgets are small.
- **Manifest check.** All four requirements pass for G2 and G5. They are European, they reduce Microsoft dependence, and they have funding routes. They are software only. The AI requirement is met only by G2. G1 alone would not qualify as an AI project.

## 4. Shortlist for the product phase

1. **G2:** an AI assistant layer for sovereign suites.
2. **G1:** migration tooling, as the entry that wins accounts.
3. **G5:** enterprise admin and compliance add-ons, for private-sector openDesk customers.
